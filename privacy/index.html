Privacy Policy

Ethos TC · Effective October 25, 2026

Ethos TC (“we,” “us”) develops Zoie, a service for discovering places and planning real-world experiences. This notice describes our intended use of information in Zoie.

Information used to provide discovery

When you search for places, Zoie may process the location or area you select, search preferences, filters, and interactions with results in order to return relevant suggestions. If you choose to permit device location, Zoie may use approximate or precise location as permitted by your device settings. You can instead select a search area manually, where available.

Google Maps Platform

Zoie uses Google Maps Platform services, including Google Places, to discover and describe locations. Search requests may transmit search terms, search-area coordinates, and related query information to Google so that it can return place information. Google's handling of information through its services is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Google-derived place information is subject to Google's applicable terms and display requirements.

Other service providers and saved data

Zoie is under development, and the services we use may change. Depending on the features made available, we may use service providers to host Zoie, support account access, store information you choose to save, operate discovery features, and maintain security and reliability. Where those services process personal information for Zoie, we will describe applicable practices in this notice as features are introduced. We do not assume that development tools automatically receive Zoie users’ personal information.

Choices and contact

You may control device-location permissions through your device settings. Other data-access, correction, and deletion options depend on the features available in the version of Zoie you use.

For privacy inquiries, contact: ryan@ethos-tc.com.

Updates

We may update this notice as Zoie evolves. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are published.
